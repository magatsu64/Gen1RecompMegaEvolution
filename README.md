# Mega Evolution in Gen 1 Recomp

All 18 Kanto species with a Mega Evolution in Pokemon Legends: Z-A (21 forms - Charizard, Raichu, and Mewtwo each have two).

Two versions:

kanto_mega_evolutions.zip — Gyarados and Clefable get their Z-A Water/Dark and Flying/Fairy typing, but needs the fairy_dark_steel_types mod.

kanto_mega_evolutions_standalone.zip — Gyarados stays Water/Flying and Clefable stays Normal/Flying instead.

How to Mega Evolve: Left on the move-select screen to select MEGA, A to open the box, confirm.

Currently both Mewtwo X/Y, Kangaskhan and Starmie are missing sprites. (Dragonite doesn't have a unique backsprite.)
