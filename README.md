# Gen1Recomp Mega Evolution

All 18 Kanto species with a Mega Evolution in Pokemon Legends: Z-A (21 forms - Charizard, Raichu, and Mewtwo each have two).

Two versions:
- `kanto_mega_evolutions.zip` — Gyarados and Clefable get their real Z-A Water/Dark and Flying/Fairy typing, but needs the `fairy_dark_steel_types` mod.
- `kanto_mega_evolutions_standalone.zip` — Gyarados stays Water/Flying and Clefable stays Normal/Flying instead, no extra dependency.

**How to Mega Evolve:** Left on the move-select screen to select MEGA, A to open the box, confirm (or pick X/Y for Charizard/Raichu/Mewtwo).

**Art:** 18 of the 21 forms have real custom Gen 1-style pixel art now; only Starmie, Kangaskhan, and Mewtwo X/Y are still placeholder recolors of the vanilla sprite.
